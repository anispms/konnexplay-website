1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. It covers three groups of people:

  1. Venue owners, managers and staff who use KonnexPlay to run a sports venue.
  2. Players who book a slot through a venue's KonnexPlay booking page.
  3. Visitors to konnexplay.com, including people who fill in the demo form or message us on WhatsApp.

2. Our role, and the venue's role

For the personal data of venue owners, staff and website visitors, KonnexPlay is the Data Fiduciary and decides how that data is used.

For the personal data of players that a venue collects through KonnexPlay, the venue is the Data Fiduciary and KonnexPlay is the Data Processor. We process that data on the venue's instructions, to provide the service. The venue decides what it collects from its players and how it contacts them, and the venue is responsible for its own lawful use of that data.

3. What we collect

From venues: business name, venue name and address, court and rate details, owner and staff names, phone numbers, email addresses, login credentials in encrypted form, bank account details for settlement, GSTIN and PAN where applicable, and your subscription and invoice history.

From players, on behalf of the venue: name, phone number, email address where given, the slot booked, the amount paid or due, payment status, booking and cancellation history.

Payment information: card, UPI and net banking details are collected and processed by our payment gateway, Razorpay. KonnexPlay does not store full card numbers, UPI PINs, CVV or bank passwords.

From website visitors: whatever you enter in the demo form (name, phone number, venue name, city, number of courts, sports, how you take bookings today, best time to call), and any message you send us by WhatsApp or email.

Automatically: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, app version, crash and error logs, and cookies or similar technology used to keep you signed in and to measure site usage.

4. Why we use it

  1. To provide the service: run your calendar, take bookings, issue confirmations, collect payments, settle money to your bank account, and send notifications.
  2. To support you: answer questions, fix problems, train your staff, and restore data.
  3. To bill you and meet our tax and accounting obligations.
  4. To keep the service safe: prevent fraud, misuse, unauthorised access and abuse.
  5. To improve the product: understand which features are used, diagnose faults, and plan changes. We use aggregated or de-identified data for this wherever possible.
  6. To send you service messages, and with your consent, occasional updates about KonnexPlay. You can opt out of the second kind at any time.

We rely on your consent, and on the legitimate uses permitted under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, including providing a service you have asked for and meeting a legal obligation.

5. Who we share it with

  1. Payment gateway Razorpay, to collect player payments and settle them to the venue.
  2. Hosting and infrastructure providers whose servers are located in India.
  3. Communication providers used to send SMS, WhatsApp messages and email on the venue's behalf.
  4. Professional advisers such as our accountants and auditors, under a duty of confidentiality.
  5. Government authorities, where we are required by law to disclose information.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share a venue's customer list with another venue, and we do not use one venue's customer data to market to players on behalf of anyone else.

When the channel manager goes live, a venue that chooses to connect a booking platform will have booking information exchanged with that platform. This will only happen for venues that switch that connection on.

6. Where data is stored

Data is stored on servers located in India. If we ever need to move data outside India, we will do so only as permitted under Indian law and we will update this policy first.

7. How long we keep it

We keep venue account data and booking records while your account is active, and after that for as long as we are required to keep financial and tax records under Indian law, which is up to eight years for books of account. Demo form enquiries that do not become customers are kept for up to 24 months. After these periods, data is deleted or anonymised.

You can ask us to delete data earlier, and we will do so unless we are required to keep it by law.

8. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you can:

  1. Ask what personal data of yours we hold and how it is processed.
  2. Ask us to correct or complete inaccurate data.
  3. Ask us to erase your data, where we are not required to keep it.
  4. Withdraw consent you have given, at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened, and may mean we can no longer provide part of the service.
  5. Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapable of doing so.
  6. Raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these, write to hello@konnexplay.com from the email or phone number registered with us. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are a player who booked at a venue and you want your data removed from that venue's list, contact the venue. If you cannot reach them, write to us and we will pass the request on and help.

9. Security

We protect data with access controls, separate logins for each staff member with limited permissions, encryption of data in transit, encrypted storage of passwords, and regular backups. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that data will never be compromised.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and the affected people within the timelines required by the DPDP Rules, and tell them what happened and what we are doing about it.

10. Children

KonnexPlay is a service for businesses and is not intended for children. A player under 18 should book only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process the personal data of a child for advertising or tracking.

11. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technology to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and measure how the site and product are used. You can block cookies in your browser, but parts of the product may stop working.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change, and for significant changes we will tell registered venues by email or in the app.

Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective date: 1 September 2026

This policy explains refunds for two different things: the KonnexPlay subscription that a venue pays us, and the booking amounts that players pay at a venue.

1. KonnexPlay subscription (paid by the venue)

  1. The subscription is an annual plan of ₹12,000 per court per year, paid in advance.
  2. Your 12-month term starts after a 14-day onboarding period, in which we set up your venue and train your staff.
  3. Once the 12-month term has started, the subscription fee is not refundable, in full or in part, whether or not the service is used.
  4. You can cancel at any time. Cancellation stops the next renewal. Your access continues until the end of the term you have paid for.
  5. To cancel, write to hello@konnexplay.com or message +91 77788 58901 from your registered number, at least 7 days before your renewal date.
  6. The ₹5,000 setup and training fee, where it applies and is not waived, is not refundable once setup at your venue has begun.
  7. If we withdraw the service, or terminate your account for a reason that is not your breach of the Terms of Service, we will refund the unused part of your subscription on a pro-rata basis.

2. Player bookings (paid by a player to a venue)

  1. Each venue sets its own cancellation and refund policy for slots, and displays it on its booking page. The booking contract is between the player and the venue.
  2. KonnexPlay does not decide whether a player's booking is refunded. The venue decides.
  3. When a venue approves a refund, it is processed through the payment gateway back to the original payment method. The money usually reaches the player within 5 to 7 working days, depending on the player's bank.
  4. Payment gateway charges on the original transaction may not be refundable. Where the gateway does not return them, they remain a cost to the venue.
  5. If a player is charged twice for the same booking, or the money is deducted but the booking did not confirm, the amount is refunded to the source account. Write to us and we will trace it with the gateway. These refunds are usually completed within 7 working days.

3. How to raise a refund request

Write to hello@konnexplay.com, or call or WhatsApp +91 77788 58901 between 9 AM and 11 PM on any day, with the venue name, the booking date and time, the amount, and the payment reference.

We acknowledge every request within 24 hours and resolve it within 15 days.
