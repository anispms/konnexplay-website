ABOUT

We lost too many games to a phone that nobody picked up.

Before KonnexPlay, one of our founders ran a box cricket community in Surat for close to five years. Every week the same thing: twelve people agree on a time at 2 AM, one person calls the venue in the morning, nobody answers, and by the time someone does, the slot has gone or moved.

On the other side of that call is an owner running four courts from a register and a memory, who has just double booked his own Saturday. We built KonnexPlay to fix both ends of that phone call.

Konnex Play Private Limited was founded in 2026 in Surat, Gujarat. KonnexPlay is used by sports venues in Gujarat and Karnataka, and is available to venues across India. Every venue is set up in person.
