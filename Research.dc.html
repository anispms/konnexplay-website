The KonnexPlay 2026 sports venue booking report.

We examined 171 sports venues in Surat to understand how they take bookings and where players can find them online. This page sets out what we did, what we found, and what it does not tell you.

Method

Date. July 2026.

Scope. 171 sports venues in Surat, Gujarat, covering box cricket arenas, football turfs, pickleball, badminton and tennis venues.

What we checked. For each venue, whether it was listed on a third-party sports booking app, and if so, on how many.

How. Desk research against public listings, supplemented by venue visits and calls by the KonnexPlay team.

Findings

Of 171 venues, only 2 were listed on more than one booking app. The remaining 169 were reachable through at most a single app, and many through none, relying on phone calls, messages and walk-ins.

A player searching on any app the venue is not on cannot see it, cannot see its availability, and cannot book it.

Limitations

This is one city, at one point in time. It does not represent the Indian market as a whole, and listings change. We did not measure booking volume, revenue or occupancy, only listing presence.

KonnexPlay is a commercial company with an interest in this topic. We publish the method so the finding can be checked.

What it means for a venue

Most Surat venues still depend on a human answering a phone to convert a player into a booking. A venue with its own booking page can be found from its Instagram, Google listing or a QR code and booked at any hour, without depending on which app a player happens to use. How KonnexPlay does this.
