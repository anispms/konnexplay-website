03 · CHANNEL MANAGER · COMING SOON

One booking updates everywhere.

Hotels solved this years ago. A hotel updates one screen, and MakeMyTrip, Booking.com and its own website all show the same room availability. Sports venues in India still do not have this. KonnexPlay is building it.

When a slot is booked on KonnexPlay, it will be blocked on every app you are listed on. When it is booked on an app, it will be blocked on KonnexPlay. No double bookings, and no more keeping five calendars by hand.

PRICING CONTROL

Change a whole season of rates before your tea gets cold.

Your morning rate, evening rate, weekend rate and festival rate are all different. Set them once as rules and every slot on every court prices itself.

All 24 hours in three clicks

Generate a full day of slots for a court, or block a range, instead of touching them one by one.

Bulk rate updates

Apply a rate to selected courts or to a whole category at once. No court-by-court editing.

Time band rates

Morning, afternoon and floodlit evening priced separately, as bands you define once.

Weekday and weekend

Every band carries a weekday price and a weekend price. Saturday prices itself.

Seasonal rate plans

Give a plan a date range for a tournament week, a festival or the monsoon. It wins over the default for those days, then expires on its own.

Rules, with priority

Base, day of week, time band and seasonal rules stack, and the most specific rule wins for a given slot.

TOURNAMENTS

A tournament weekend, without losing the rest of the month.

Tournaments are the fastest growing thing at Indian venues and the hardest to run on a register. Sixteen teams, two days, every court blocked, a different rate, deposits to collect and refund, and regular players who still want their Sunday slot.

KonnexPlay handles the mechanics, so a tournament costs you a weekend of work instead of a month of it.

Block the whole weekend in three clicks. Generate and block every slot across every court for the tournament dates, instead of marking ninety-six slots by hand.
Put a tournament rate on those dates only. A seasonal rate plan with a date range overrides your normal price for the event, then expires by itself.
Collect team deposits online. Take a deposit per team at registration, so a team that drops out does not cost you the slot.
Give the discounts you promised. Discount a sponsor team or an early entry, and see exactly what was discounted in the payment report.
Add extra charges cleanly. Lights, balls, referees, cafe or equipment go on the booking, not on a scrap of paper.
Know what the tournament made. Filter revenue, payments and expenses to those dates and read the profit, not a feeling.
Every team captain who registers is saved to your customer list, so next season's invitation is one message away.
